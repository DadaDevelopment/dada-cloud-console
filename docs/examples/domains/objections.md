# Handling Client Objections

## Overview

Common objections during onboarding and how to address them professionally while maintaining compliance. Use this when client:
- Resists providing required information
- Questions platform legitimacy
- Compares unfavorably to competitors
- Expresses urgency to bypass steps

## Objection Categories

### 1. "This is too complicated / too many steps"

**Root cause:** Client expects instant access (crypto exchange pattern)

**Response framework:**
"I understand you want to start trading quickly. Our process is:
- [Specific time]: KYC review in 1-3 business days
- [Reason]: Required by financial regulators to protect both you and the platform
- [Benefit]: Once approved, unlimited trading with no delays

Would you like me to walk you through the fastest path?"

**Fastest path:**
1. Complete KYC now (documents ready)
2. Deposit during review (funds ready when approved)
3. Start trading within 24-48h (expedited for $10k+ deposits)

### 2. "Other platforms don't ask for this"

**Root cause:** Client comparing to unregulated/offshore platforms

**Response framework:**
"You're right that some platforms have lighter requirements. Here's the difference:
- **Unregulated platforms**: no KYC, but higher risk of scams, frozen funds, no legal recourse
- **Our platform**: FCA-regulated, your funds protected by [protection scheme], full legal rights

We prioritize your security over speed. Many clients appreciate that after hearing horror stories from unregulated platforms."

**Do not:**
- Name specific competitor platforms
- Claim competitors are illegal (even if true)
- Promise to "make an exception"

### 3. "I need to trade NOW, can you skip verification?"

**Root cause:** FOMO, market movement, or urgency scam attempt

**Response framework:**
"I understand the market is moving and you want to act fast. Unfortunately:
- Regulatory compliance is non-negotiable (we can lose our license)
- No exceptions, even for high-value clients
- Standard review: 1-3 days; expedited (24h) available for $10k+ deposits

**Alternative:** You can complete registration now, and I'll escalate your KYC for expedited review. That's the fastest legally possible path."

**Red flag:** Client insists on bypassing verification or offers "extra fee" to skip → escalate to compliance (possible fraud attempt)

### 4. "Why do you need my address/salary/source of funds?"

**Root cause:** Privacy concerns, or hiding non-legitimate source

**Response framework (legitimate privacy concern):**
"These questions are required by anti-money laundering (AML) regulations:
- **Address**: verify your jurisdiction and tax residency
- **Source of funds**: ensure money isn't from illegal activity
- **Salary**: assess appropriate account tier and risk limits

Your data is encrypted, GDPR-protected, and never sold to third parties."

**Red flag:** Evasive answers, "I don't want to say," or contradictory information → escalate to compliance

### 5. "This platform looks like a scam"

**Root cause:** Client previously scammed, or due diligence before trusting

**Response framework (validate their concern):**
"Smart to be cautious—there are scams in this industry. Here's how to verify we're legitimate:
- **Regulation**: FCA license #[NUMBER], searchable on FCA register
- **Company registration**: [COMPANY NAME], Companies House registration [NUMBER]
- **Independent reviews**: Trustpilot [rating], media coverage [links]
- **Office address**: [physical address], you can visit or call

Would you like me to send verification links, or do you have specific concerns I can address?"

**Do not:** Get defensive or dismissive—validate their caution as smart behavior.

## Escalation Triggers

Escalate immediately if client:
- Threatens legal action or regulator complaint
- Becomes abusive or aggressive
- Admits to being from restricted jurisdiction
- Offers bribe/incentive to bypass rules
- Claims PEP status or government affiliation

**Escalation script:**
"I want to make sure you get the best possible assistance. Let me connect you with our [compliance team / senior account manager] who can address your specific situation."
